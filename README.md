## The project

Commercial kitchens run on tight margins. The plan proposes a company that supplies
IoT-enabled kitchen equipment (such as a connected dishwasher), installs it on site, and
supports it remotely. The business case is weighed over five years.

The work was split into six departments, two per team member:

| Department | Covers |
|---|---|
| **HR** | Recruitment, safety training, employee wellbeing, training and development |
| **R&D** | Designing and prototyping the IoT components; integration with the installation team |
| **Project Manufacturing** | Component manufacturing, quality assurance, IoT hardware, production |
| **Project Support** | Pre-install site assessment, user training, post-install remote monitoring and maintenance |
| **Technical Installation** | Equipment placement, plumbing, electrical, network connectivity, on-site testing |
| **Marketing** | Market research, campaigns, trade shows, customer outreach |

My own planning files (in [`planning/`](planning/)) cover **HR and R&D**: the HR work
breakdown structure, the Microsoft Project schedule with its resource sheet, and the
five-year wage costing for those roles.

## What the plan includes

- **Scope and approach:**
  - objectives for each department
  - stakeholder identification and communication
  - contractual aspects (reporting, liaison, review and cancellation)
- **Planning and scheduling:**
  - a mind map
  - work breakdown structures and action plans with milestones for every department
  - Gantt-chart schedules
- **Resourcing:** staff allocation tables with hours and costs for each role across five years.
- **Cost analysis:**
  - payback of just over 3.5 years on an initial investment of €813,000
  - a net present value analysis with 2% inflation
  - a profitability index of 5.65
  - a return on investment of 37.91%
- **Risk:** operational, IoT security, customer acquisition and HR risks, each with a mitigation.
- **Evaluation and control:**
  - milestone tracking
  - quality assurance
  - budget monitoring
  - change control
  - IoT uptime and data-accuracy metrics
